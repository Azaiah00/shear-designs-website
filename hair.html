Before you book

A few house rules.

Consult by text

After requesting on Square, you will receive consultation questions by text. Respond within 24 hours.

10-minute grace period

Running late? Let the salon know. Without notice, late arrivals are cancelled as a no-show.

No guests

Feeling unwell?

Please stay home if you are sick, and reach out to reschedule.

FAQ

Hair questions.

Do you style long hair?

No. Shear Designs offers short hairstyling only: pixies, tapers, molds, finger waves, wave sets and designs.

I am new. Which service do I book?

All new guests book the New Client Package (Virgin Relaxer / Retouch / Cut / Style) for $205. It is required for your first visit.

Can I get a relaxer if I have permanent color?

The New Client Package is not available to clients with permanent color. Call or text (804) 965-3210 with questions before you book.

How short does my hair need to be for a wave set?

The Shampoo / Wave Set & Curl ($85) is for hair 3 inches or less, with a relaxer within the last 2 weeks.

Who can book Color POP or Color Me Rainbow?

Color POP ($95) and the Color Me Rainbow package ($150, up to 3 vivids) are for established clients.
