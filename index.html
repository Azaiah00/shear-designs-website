FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you only do short hair?

Yes. Shear Designs is a short-hair-only salon: pixie cuts, relaxer retouches, molds, finger waves, wave sets, designs and color for short styles. Long hair services are not offered.

What should I book as a new client?

New guests start with the New Client Package (Virgin Relaxer / Retouch / Cut / Style), $205, about 1 hr 40 min. A camouflage cut for thinning hair may add $40. Clients with permanent color cannot be accepted for this package.

How do I book an appointment?

Request your time on Square. After you request, you will get a text with consultation questions; reply within 24 hours to keep your appointment request active.

How much do ombré brows cost?

Ombré brows (microshading) are $395, take about 2 hr 25 min and last up to 3 years. A $50 deposit is required. See the brows page for touch-up pricing.

How do I pay a deposit?

Deposits can be sent by Cash App to $HairandbrowsRVA, or by Zelle or Apple Pay to 804-965-3210.

Where do I park?

There is free lot parking at the salon, at 9501 Amberdale Dr, Richmond, VA 23236, beside Sheetz and under the big clock.

Can I bring a friend or my kids?

No. It is a private salon and no guests are allowed inside, so please come to your appointment alone.

What if I am running late?

There is a 10-minute grace period if you let the salon know you are running late. Without notice, a late appointment is cancelled as a no-show.

What are your hours?

Monday 9:30 am to 4 pm, Tuesday and Wednesday 9 am to 4 pm, Thursday 9:30 am to 6 pm, Friday 9:30 am to 4:30 pm, Saturday 7 am to 12 pm. Closed Sunday.

Do you offer brow training?

Yes. Shawntae teaches private one-on-one Microshading (Ombré) and Microblading courses, from $1,095 for a 1-day class. Details are on the Academy page.
