Ombré explained

What is microshading?

Ombré brows are a semi-permanent eyebrow tattoo. Pigment is shaded into the skin in fine layers for a soft, powdered finish that looks like beautifully filled-in brows, every day, without the pencil.

Ombré brows · $395 · about 2 hr 25 min · lasts up to 3 years

Front: softTail: defined
01 · Map

Shaped to your face

Every set starts with mapping, so the arch, length and thickness suit your features before any pigment goes in.

02 · Shade

Light to deep, front to tail

The shading stays airy at the front of the brow and builds toward the arch and tail. That gradient is the “ombré.” The full service takes about 2 hr 25 min.

03 · Heal & perfect

Touch-up at 6–8 weeks

Brows go through a few dramatic healing stages. The touch-up refines everything, and results last up to 3 years.

The healing journey

Six stages to perfect.

Shawntae’s own healing chart, so nothing surprises you on the way to your finished brows.

  1. Day 1“Loving my new brows!”Fresh, crisp and fully shaded.
  2. Day 2“OMG, my brows are too dark!”Completely normal. Color sits boldest right after the service.
  3. Days 5–10“Scabbing and itchy.”Light flaking as the skin heals. Let it shed on its own.
  4. 2–3 weeks“Where’s my tattoo?”Brows can look faded while the new skin settles over the pigment.
  5. 3–5 weeks“The color is coming back.”Pigment resurfaces and softens into its true tone.
  6. After touch-up“My BeautyFULL brows are perfect!”The 6–8 week touch-up refines shape and color.

FAQ

Brow questions.

How long do ombré brows last?

Ombré brows (microshading) last up to 3 years. Regular touch-ups keep the color fresh; an annual color boost is $250.

How much are ombré brows and is there a deposit?

The full ombré brow service is $395 and takes about 2 hr 25 min. A $50 deposit is required to book, sent by Cash App, Zelle or Apple Pay.

When should I book my touch-up?

The first touch-up is best at 6 to 8 weeks ($125). Pricing rises the longer you wait: $145 at 8 to 10 weeks, up to $195 at 16 to 20 weeks.

I had my brows tattooed by someone else. Can I book?

Yes, but previous work by another artist requires the Ombré Brow Cover-Up / Correction service ($500, $50 deposit) instead of the standard service.

Why do my brows look so dark after the appointment?

That is a normal healing stage. Brows look darkest around day 2, scab and itch around days 5 to 10, may seem to disappear at 2 to 3 weeks, and color returns at 3 to 5 weeks.

Can I get ombré brows while pregnant or nursing?

No. The service is not offered to pregnant or nursing clients, or to clients with serious skin conditions or severe acne.

How should I prepare for my brow appointment?

Avoid caffeine, alcohol and pain medication for 24 hours before your appointment.
