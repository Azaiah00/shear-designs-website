Meet Shawntae.

Shawntae Dupree Woolfolk is a licensed cosmetologist who specializes in short hair and ombré brows. She founded Shear Designs as an upscale, private salon, and her community calls itself Pixie Nation.

I am a licensed cosmetologist, and have always loved styling hair and transforming ladies to enhance their natural beauty.

Shear Designs is an upscale beauty salon providing trendy short (only) hairstyling services and semi-permanent brows and training in a private salon located in Richmond, VA.

Her brow work lives under its own name, BeautyFULL Brows PMU Artistry, and she teaches it one-on-one through the Academy. Clients travel from across Virginia and beyond for her cuts and brows.

The private salon

Private, relaxing, yours.

A private salon means no extra guests inside: just you, Shawntae and the work. Clients describe a relaxing, professional environment and praise the atmosphere and privacy.

  • Private, appointment-only salon
  • Free lot parking beside Sheetz, under the big clock
  • Short hair and brows only, done at Master level

Salon policies

Good to know before you book.

New guests

New guests must book the Virgin Relaxer Package (New Client Package, $205) for their first appointment.

Consultation text

After you request an appointment, you will get a text with consultation questions. Please respond within 24 hours.

Grace period

You have a 10-minute grace period if you notify the salon. Otherwise the appointment is cancelled as a no-show.

No guests

No guests are allowed inside the salon.

Health

Please stay home if you are sick.

Deposits

Send deposits by Cash App ($HairandbrowsRVA), or Zelle / Apple Pay to 804-965-3210.
