FAQ

Academy questions.

How much is microshading or microblading training?

The private one-on-one course is $1,095 for 1 day (9:30 am to 4:30 pm) or $1,695 for 2 days, with day 2 spent on a live model from 10 am to 2 pm.

What is included in the course?

A starter kit, tattoo machine, hands-on practice, 3 shading techniques, bloodborne pathogens certification, safety procedures, insurance information, marketing and branding strategies, certification, lunch and continuing mentorship.

How do I reserve a training date?

A $200 non-refundable deposit by Cash App, Zelle or Apple Pay reserves your spot. The balance is due at the start of class in cash or Zelle.

Is training one-on-one?

Yes. All Academy courses are private, one-on-one sessions with Shawntae.

Do previous students get a discount?

Yes. Prior students receive $50 off the Refresher Course, available to students whose intro course was within the last 24 months.
